Careers at Frostline

Build a career in commercial HVAC engineering with a company that invests in its people.

Work with us

Frostline is a growing regional HVAC contractor with 41 permanent staff. We value practical competence, professional development and people who take ownership of their work.

We employ installation engineers, service engineers, apprentices, coordinators, estimators, project managers and support staff. Whether you are starting your career or looking for your next step, we offer genuine progression in a company where your contribution is visible and valued.

Our engineers work across a varied portfolio of commercial sites — offices, hotels, schools, retail, healthcare and industrial premises. No two days are quite the same, and the work is technically interesting.

Why Frostline

Genuine career progression

Clear pathways from apprentice to improver, engineer, senior engineer, lead or management roles. Progression is based on capability and contribution, not time served.

Structured training

Manufacturer training, formal qualifications, supervised field development and technical mentoring. We invest in building capability that makes you more effective and more valuable.

Varied, interesting work

Commercial air-conditioning, ventilation, refrigeration, heat pumps and controls across a wide range of building types. Technically diverse and rarely repetitive.

A practical, supportive culture

We value sound engineering judgement and people who are trusted to make good decisions. Asking for help is a sign of professionalism, not weakness.

Regional, not national

Most work is within a reasonable travel radius of Greater Manchester. You will get home at a sensible time and build knowledge of the sites and customers you support.

Independent ownership

Frostline is owned by its directors, not a private-equity group or national chain. Decisions are made by people who work in the business and know the team personally.

Career pathways

Frostline develops people through practical experience supported by formal training.

Apprenticeships

We take apprentices seriously. You will work under planned supervision, gaining progressive exposure to installation, service, fault finding, documentation and customer communication. College study is combined with genuine hands-on development — not making tea and carrying tools.

Engineering roles

Our installation and service engineers work on commercial HVAC equipment across the North West. Roles range from routine maintenance and reactive repair through to complex diagnostics, commissioning and project delivery. We match work to your competence and develop it further.

Senior and specialist roles

Experienced engineers can progress to senior, lead or specialist positions — including commissioning, controls, technical mentoring, estimating and project management. We value depth as well as breadth.

Interested in joining Frostline?

We are always interested in hearing from capable engineers and motivated people looking to build a career in commercial HVAC.
