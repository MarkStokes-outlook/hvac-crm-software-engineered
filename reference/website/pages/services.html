Our Services

Complete commercial HVAC engineering — maintenance, repair, installation and ongoing support.

Planned Maintenance

Regular, scheduled servicing keeps your HVAC equipment safe, reliable and efficient. We design maintenance plans around your equipment, your building and your operational needs — not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

A typical maintenance visit includes inspection, cleaning, operational testing, temperature and pressure readings, filter attention, refrigerant checks and identification of emerging faults. We record findings and let you know when something needs attention before it becomes a breakdown.

What you get:

  • Scheduled visits at an agreed frequency
  • Documented service records and compliance evidence
  • Early identification of deterioration and defects
  • Recommendations with evidence, not assumptions
  • Continuity — the same team building knowledge of your site

Maintenance contracts range from a handful of units at one site to portfolios covering many sites and several hundred items of equipment.

Reactive Service & Repair

When your heating or cooling fails, you need a prompt, organised response — not just someone turning up and hoping for the best. Frostline coordinates reactive work through our service team, keeping you informed from the first call through to resolution.

We assess urgency, consider your contract and site constraints, and arrange attendance by an engineer with the right skills for your equipment. The aim is a proper diagnosis and a lasting solution, not a quick patch that fails again next week.

Our approach:

  • Coordinated response with clear communication
  • Proper fault diagnosis — not just alarm-clearing
  • Transparent commercial treatment from the start
  • Follow-through on parts, return visits and resolution
  • Out-of-hours support for contracted customers

We know reactive work rarely ends with a single visit. When parts are needed, specialist access is required or further investigation is warranted, we tell you what is happening, what comes next and when.

Installation & Projects

Frostline designs and installs air-conditioning, ventilation, heat-pump and associated control systems for commercial premises. Projects range from single-unit replacements completed in a day to multi-zone installations spanning several weeks.

We handle survey, design coordination, equipment selection, procurement, installation, testing, commissioning and handover. On larger projects we work alongside main contractors and other trades, aligning with site programmes and access requirements.

Typical projects include:

  • Office and retail fit-out air-conditioning
  • Ventilation upgrades for schools, kitchens and care facilities
  • Multi-zone VRF and split-system installations
  • Plant room replacements and system modernisation
  • Heat-pump installations and low-carbon transitions

Every installation is commissioned, documented and handed over properly. We also provide ongoing maintenance so your new equipment is looked after from day one.

Replacement & Upgrades

Equipment reaches the end of its useful life for many reasons — age, reliability, refrigerant availability, energy performance or changing building use. We help you plan replacements sensibly, not reactively.

Our maintenance engineers often identify replacement needs during routine servicing, giving you time to plan and budget rather than replacing in a crisis. We advise on modern alternatives, efficiency improvements and practical considerations such as phased installation, temporary arrangements and minimal disruption.

We can help with:

  • Like-for-like equipment replacement
  • System upgrades for efficiency or capacity
  • Refrigerant phase-out replacements
  • Controls modernisation
  • Phased programmes across multiple sites

Additional capabilities

Beyond our core services, we support our customers with:

Commissioning

Testing and commissioning of new and replacement systems, including systems installed by others where scope is clearly defined.

Warranty support

Investigation of faults during warranty periods, managing manufacturer claims and ensuring you are not left without support.

Emergency response

Out-of-hours support for contracted customers with business-critical failures. Telephone advice, attendance or make-safe as the situation requires.

Specialist coordination

We work with approved subcontractors for scaffolding, specialist electrical work, lifting, controls integration and other trades outside our direct scope.
