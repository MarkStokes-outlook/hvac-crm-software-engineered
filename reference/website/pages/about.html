About Frostline

An independently owned commercial HVAC contractor, established in 2011.

Who we are

Frostline Mechanical Services is a privately owned commercial HVAC contractor headquartered in Greater Manchester. We provide installation, planned maintenance, reactive repair and replacement services for heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and refrigeration equipment.

We work primarily across Greater Manchester, Lancashire, Merseyside, Cheshire and West Yorkshire, supporting commercial and public-sector customers who need dependable engineering and a practical, long-term service relationship.

Our story

Frostline was founded in 2011 by refrigeration engineers Martin Hale and Susan Mercer. Both had worked for larger regional contractors and believed there was room for a smaller firm that combined competent engineering with direct customer service.

The company began with the founders, one apprentice and a rented industrial unit in Bury. Early work focused on small commercial air-conditioning installations, refrigeration repairs and subcontract support.

Since then we have grown steadily through repeat business and referrals, expanding into planned maintenance, larger replacement projects, ventilation upgrades and multi-site service contracts. In 2021 we acquired the customer contracts of Calder Cooling Services, expanding our presence in West Yorkshire.

How we work

Frostline is large enough to run distinct service, installation, procurement and coordination functions, but small enough that senior managers remain closely involved in day-to-day delivery.

We employ 41 permanent staff across field engineering, service coordination, installation management, estimating, procurement, finance and administration. We also work regularly with a small pool of approved subcontract engineers and specialist trades.

Our strongest customer relationships combine installation work with ongoing maintenance. We value service continuity because it builds knowledge of your sites, your equipment and your expectations over time.

What matters to us

Practical engineering

We value competence, reliability and the ability to solve problems under real site conditions. Our people are trusted to make sound decisions.

Customer continuity

We prefer long-term relationships to one-off transactions. Many of our customers have worked with the same coordinators and engineers for years.

Ownership and honesty

Staff take ownership of problems. We are straightforward about what we can deliver, and we follow through until the job is properly resolved.

Responsible growth

We want to remain an accessible, responsive company. Growth is measured and deliberate — we do not take on work we cannot deliver well.
