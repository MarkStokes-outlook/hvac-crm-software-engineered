# Discovery 002 — Agency / FrostLine SME transcript

Preserved as a working conversation, not rewritten into a perfect questionnaire.

## Session A — customers, sites and records

**DISC-Q001 Agency:** What are the core things staff mean by customer, site and job? **SME:** A customer is the commercial organisation; a site is the physical service location; a job is the operational container for service/small works. One customer can have many sites and the payer, occupier and equipment owner can differ. An attendance is one intervention within a job and does not itself close the job.

**DISC-Q002 Agency:** What customer information matters? **SME:** Trading name helps operations, but finance needs the correct legal/billing entity. Contacts have different roles—procurement, facilities, site, finance, technical, escalation—and the fault reporter may not be authorised to spend. Multi-site customers can have different PO/invoice arrangements.

**DISC-Q003 Agency:** What belongs on a site? **SME:** Address, contacts, opening hours, parking/loading, keys/security, inductions/permits, roof/plant access, asbestos information, safeguarding and restrictions on disruptive work. Coordinators often reconfirm access because it changes.

**DISC-Q004 Agency:** Asset history or site history? **SME:** Both. Engineers need relevant system/asset history; part identification often needs manufacturer, model, serial and internal asset ID. Equipment ownership can differ from site occupier.

**DISC-Q005 Agency:** Projects and jobs—same thing? **SME:** No. A project is a managed body of work with a defined outcome and coordination requirement; a job normally contains service/small works. They share customers/sites/assets/costs/attendances but project delivery has longer planning and different commercial milestones.

## Session B — service, SLA and scheduling

**DISC-Q006 Agency:** Walk us through a reactive call. **SME:** Receive the symptom, identify customer/site/asset as far as possible, check contract/authority, assess impact/safety, decide priority and remote triage, then the required attendance. Diagnosis may be the first authorised outcome rather than repair.

**DISC-Q007 Agency:** How is priority decided? **SME:** By current impact, not who shouts loudest. We commonly use P1 critical, P2 high, P3 normal, P4 planned. Consider safety/property risk, service loss, critical areas, backup, stock/medicine exposure, weather, hours and contractual criticality. Record reason/time/decision-maker for changes.

**DISC-Q008 Agency:** Which SLA timestamps matter? **SME:** Request received, acknowledgement, initial response, dispatched, attendance, diagnosis, restoration, resolution and closure are distinct. Attendance means a suitable engineer reaches the agreed service location and can reasonably begin. Restoration can be temporary; resolution is the agreed remedial outcome.

**DISC-Q009 Agency:** Can SLA clocks pause? **SME:** Only where the contract permits and a genuine dependency prevents progress—no access, permit, customer delay/authority, unsafe external condition, utilities, third party, manufacturer decision or parts. Record reason, start, evidence, expected actor, FrostLine owner, chase point and restart. Never add stops retrospectively to improve performance.

**DISC-Q010 Agency:** How do planners assign engineers? **SME:** Balance priority, competence/authorisation, manufacturer experience, certifications, site clearance, geography/travel, fatigue/working time, parts/tools, duration and existing promises. Nearest/free is not enough. Diary entries may be provisional; distinguish customer-confirmed commitments.

**DISC-Q011 Agency:** What makes work schedulable? **SME:** We distinguish received, triaged, authorised, ready, scheduled and dispatched. Normal work needs sufficient scope, authority, access, competence, parts/resources and dependencies. Emergencies can deliberately proceed with uncertainty.

**DISC-Q012 Agency:** Emergency displaces other work—what survives? **SME:** Why, who authorised reprioritisation, affected work, customer communications and a new owner/next action for displaced work.

**DISC-Q013 Agency:** How should waiting work appear? **SME:** Not generic “on hold”. State the dependency—customer approval, part availability, manufacturer decision, technical review, access, subcontract acceptance, or ready to schedule—with owner and review/chase trigger.

## Session C — engineer attendance

**DISC-Q014 Agency:** What does the engineer need before travel? **SME:** Customer/site/service location, contacts/access, symptom, authorised scope, asset/history, prior notes, quote/contract entitlement or spend limit, priority/commitments, hazards/method info, permits/inductions/shutdowns, parts/tools/test equipment and duration.

**DISC-Q015 Agency:** Can an engineer reject/stop? **SME:** Escalate material gaps in scope, competence, equipment, working time, access or duration. On site, anyone may stop work reasonably believed unsafe or outside competence. Customer urgency does not override that.

**DISC-Q016 Agency:** Can they just fix what they find? **SME:** Site presence is not unlimited authority. Basis may be diagnosis only, maintenance, accepted repair, contract-covered minor repair, delegated spend, project task, warranty investigation/remediation or emergency make-safe. Separate technical recommendation, customer authority, FrostLine spend authority and resource availability.

**DISC-Q017 Agency:** Attendance cannot finish—then what? **SME:** Give it an honest outcome: partial, diagnosis complete/further work, temporary restoration, made safe, no fault found, no access, missing parts/tools, changed scope/authority, specialist required, etc. Job can remain open. Handoff needs required outcome, evidence, exact dependency, safety/operating condition, parts/specialist need, promises, urgency, authority and recommended next owner.

**DISC-Q018 Agency:** Evidence? **SME:** Notes, labour/travel, parts/materials, readings, commissioning, photos, acknowledgement, certificates, recommendations and non-completion reason as relevant. Separate reported symptom, observed facts, tests, hypothesis, verified diagnosis, work, final condition, uncertainty, approvals and follow-on.

**DISC-Q019 Agency:** Temporary repair? **SME:** Future obligation, not “fixed”. Record change/reason, restored service, limits/residual risk, monitoring, expiry/review, customer understanding, approver and permanent-resolution owner.

**DISC-Q020 Agency:** Does customer sign-off close/approve charges? **SME:** No; it acknowledges attendance/outcome, not unquoted charges, warranty responsibility, design or final closure.

**DISC-Q021 Agency:** When is a job closed? **SME:** Operational completion, financial closure and commercial/warranty resolution are separable. Don't make one complete flag erase those distinctions.

## Session D — quotation and release

**DISC-Q022 Agency:** Quote journey? **SME:** Enquiry/opportunity, qualification, survey/scope, estimate, internal approval, issued quote/revision, acceptance validation, commercial release and handover. Budget indication, concept estimate, developed estimate, approved quotation basis and delivery forecast have different confidence/meaning.

**DISC-Q023 Agency:** Formal quote contents? **SME:** Customer/site, number/revision, scope, equipment/materials, programme/lead time, price/VAT, payment terms, validity, assumptions/exclusions, warranty position, customer responsibilities and acceptance method; add outage, design responsibility, provisional sums/options/dependencies where relevant.

**DISC-Q024 Agency:** What turns “yes” into accepted work? **SME:** Verify accepting party/authority, exact revision/options, PO/value, customer terms, achievable dates, validity/supplier pricing and credit/deposit conditions. Approval to issue is not authority to purchase/start.

**DISC-Q025 Agency:** Revisions/variations? **SME:** Preserve revision history. After award classify change as in-scope clarification, FrostLine error, customer change, failed explicit assumption, third-party dependency, emergency make-safe or separate follow-on. Discovery is not approval.

**DISC-Q026 Agency:** Exact monetary thresholds by role? **SME:** Don't invent them here. Make approval policy configurable and require identifiable approver/reason; governance decision needed before production.

## Session E — warehouse/materials

**DISC-Q027 Agency:** Is stock quantity-on-hand? **SME:** No. Physical stock can be available, reserved, picked, issued, quarantined, return-pending, evidence-held, obsolete/disposed, customer-owned or project-specific. Availability must exclude illegitimate issues.

**DISC-Q028 Agency:** Reservation fields? **SME:** Item/qty, intended work/customer/purpose, requester, accountable owner, required/reservation dates, review/expiry, substitution permission and consequence of reallocation. Reallocation requires review and notification.

**DISC-Q029 Agency:** Damaged goods receipt? **SME:** Record supplier/PO/date/carrier/items/qty/condition/allocation. Separate uncertain goods from available stock, capture evidence, notify procurement, record operational impact, next action and return deadline. Carrier signature isn't technical acceptance.

**DISC-Q030 Agency:** Van issues/returns? **SME:** Van stock is distributed inventory. Record work, recipient/van/site, item/qty, serial/batch where needed, time/source and return expectation. Assess returns before availability—unused/opened/damaged/contaminated/suspect/customer-owned/warranty/return/disposal.

**DISC-Q031 Agency:** Failed parts for warranty? **SME:** Preserve chain of custody: source customer/site/system/asset, job/case, removal date/engineer, failure evidence, photos/tests, condition/packaging, storage, deadline, manufacturer/supplier ref and next-action owner. Don't strip/scrap until authorised.

## Session F — reporting, permissions, AI and delivery

**DISC-Q032 Agency:** What should the operations dashboard answer? **SME:** What is urgent, what is due/at risk, what is waiting and on whom, which appointments are committed, what work is ready but unscheduled, and where SLA/maintenance promises may breach. Avoid vanity averages without denominator/time basis.

**DISC-Q033 Agency:** Who are the main user groups? **SME:** Service coordinators/planners, field engineers, sales/estimating, installation/project staff, procurement/warehouse, finance and managers/directors. People need different views and authority; an engineer should not silently approve commercial changes just because they can edit a job.

**DISC-Q034 Agency:** What auditability matters? **SME:** Consequential changes should retain who/when/reason and relevant before/after context—priority, authority, schedule displacement, approvals, stock state/reallocation, clock stops and closure decisions especially.

**DISC-Q035 Agency:** Where would AI genuinely help? **SME:** Summarising history, surfacing relevant prior work/asset context, drafting customer updates or engineer-note summaries, suggesting triage questions and highlighting missing handoff information. It should not invent diagnosis, accept warranty liability, authorise spend, change SLA clocks or make safety decisions without human review.

**DISC-Q036 Agency:** Any non-functional expectations? **SME:** Engineers need a fast mobile-friendly workflow; office staff need dense desktop views. Search/history must be quick enough for live calls. Protect customer/site/security information, use role-based access, audit important actions, validate inputs, and don't lose submitted field evidence. Exact hosting/SSO/retention are not decided in this discovery.

**DISC-Q037 Agency:** Do we need a customer portal or offline app now? **SME:** Not established. Don't assume either for V1. Responsive web is acceptable for the handover unless later delivery constraints prove otherwise.

**DISC-Q038 Agency:** What would make V1 useful rather than a demo? **SME:** A coordinator can log/triage work, see customer/site/asset history, schedule a suitable engineer, engineer can execute and hand back evidence/follow-on, office can control open dependencies, quotes can reach controlled acceptance/release, and stock can be reserved/issued without lying about availability. Reporting should derive from those records.

**DISC-Q039 Agency:** Data migration/integrations? **SME:** We know information currently lives in spreadsheets/inboxes and finance has its own concerns, but we haven't scoped migration or accounting integration. Provide import/export seams, don't fabricate integrations.

**DISC-Q040 Agency:** Anything we haven't asked that blocks design? **SME:** Exact authority thresholds, detailed warranty/project/purchasing/finance statutory workflows and production platform decisions remain open. Design V1 so those policies can be extended without pretending they are known.
