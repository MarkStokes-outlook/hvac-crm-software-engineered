# Discovery 001 — Initial workshop

**Inputs:** frozen stakeholder brief and public website only.

The agency understands FrostLine as a growing commercial HVAC contractor whose operational coordination increasingly depends on spreadsheets, inboxes, calls and staff memory. The requested product is an internal operational CRM covering customer/site context, work, scheduling, engineer activity, assets/history, quotations, parts/stock, operational status and useful AI assistance.

The initial material does not define a complete domain/state model, permissions matrix, approval policy or architecture. Discovery is therefore time-boxed into customers/sites/assets; service/SLA/scheduling; field delivery; commercial/quoting; stock/materials; and reporting/security/technical delivery. Questions not asked remain gaps.
