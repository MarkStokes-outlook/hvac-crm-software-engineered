# Discovery decisions

- **DISC-D001:** Treat customer, site, asset, job/project and attendance as separate concepts.
- **DISC-D002:** Model lifecycle events explicitly; avoid a single overloaded `status`/`complete` flag.
- **DISC-D003:** Every waiting state must carry dependency, owner and review/chase point.
- **DISC-D004:** Scheduling is readiness + competence + commitments, not merely free diary slots.
- **DISC-D005:** Engineer mobile workflow must preserve reported facts, observed facts, authority, safety condition, evidence and follow-on.
- **DISC-D006:** Commercial acceptance and operational release are separate from quotation issue/approval.
- **DISC-D007:** Inventory availability must respect reservation, quarantine, ownership and evidence custody.
- **DISC-D008:** Monetary approval thresholds remain configurable/unresolved; do not invent them.
- **DISC-D009:** AI is advisory. It may summarise, retrieve and draft, but consequential state/authority decisions require attributable human action.
- **DISC-D010:** Discovery closes after the six planned sessions. No later canonical lookup is permitted for engineering.
